## Where do I find my room?

Your coach has your room assignments. The [map](/map/) has floor plans for every building. If you still can't find it, ask any volunteer.

## Where are schedule changes posted?

On [live updates](/updates/) and on the [schedule](/schedule/), where changed times are shown in red. Both pages refresh themselves.

## Can I use a calculator?

No. Calculators are not permitted on any round.

## Where is lunch?

Pre-ordered lunches are picked up in Kerr Lobby in the MLK Student Union. Nearby restaurants are listed on the [map](/map/) page and along Telegraph Avenue and in Downtown Berkeley.

## Where do parents and coaches go during rounds?

Parents and coaches are welcome in Dwinelle Hall and the MLK Student Union. Watch [live updates](/updates/) for parent tours and talks.

## Where is the awards ceremony?

Awards happen in several auditoriums at the same time. Go to the room assigned at check-in; a different room may delay your award.

## I lost something.

Use the [lost & found](/lost-found/) form, or visit the information desk at Dwinelle.

## Where's the information desk?

In Dwinelle Hall by the main entrance (floor D). Any volunteer can point you there.

## Who do I contact for an emergency?

For an immediate emergency, call 911 and alert a nearby staff member. For non-immediate ones, email [team@berkeley.mt](mailto:team@berkeley.mt) or find any volunteer. See [help](/help/).
