## Before you arrive

- Coaches check in at **Dwinelle 145** when doors open; see the [schedule](/schedule/) for times. Teams that checked in online pick up materials at Dwinelle 155.
- Bring pencils, erasers and a water bottle. **No calculators** are allowed on any round.
- Agree on a meeting point with your coach or family, and plan your journey with [TakeTransit](https://taketransit.berkeley.mt/).

## Finding your room

Your coach has your room assignment for each round. Use the [map](/map/) to find the building and floor plan. Room numbers in Dwinelle tell you the floor (1xx is floor D at the main entrance, 2xx is floor E). Volunteers in BMT shirts can point you the right way.

## During the day

- **Follow your proctor's instructions.** A scheduled start time is not a signal to begin.
- Check [live updates](/updates/) for schedule changes and announcements. The page refreshes itself.
- Stay in your assigned room between rounds unless told otherwise. Keep your belongings with you.

## Lunch

Pre-ordered lunches are picked up in Kerr Lobby (MLK Student Union). There are plenty of restaurants on the south side of campus and in Downtown Berkeley. Be back in your room before the afternoon round starts.

## Awards

Awards are presented simultaneously in several auditoriums. Report to the awards room you were assigned at check-in; going to a different room may delay your award.
