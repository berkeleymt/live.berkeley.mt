{% alert(status="error", title="Emergency") %}
For an immediate emergency, **call 911** and alert the nearest BMT staff member. Online forms are not monitored for emergencies.
{% end %}

## Information desk

In Dwinelle Hall by the main entrance (floor D). Come here for directions, room assignments, lost items and anything else. Ask any volunteer in a BMT shirt to point you there.

## Wi-Fi

Connect to **CalVisitor**, UC Berkeley's free guest network. No password is needed; accept the terms in the browser window that opens.

## Restrooms

Every floor plan on the [map](/map/) marks restrooms in blue, including gender-neutral restrooms. Ask a volunteer for the nearest accessible restroom.

## Accessibility

Every tournament building has an elevator (marked ☒ on the floor plans). If a route is closed or you need step-free access, ask a volunteer or come to the information desk.

## Lost something?

Use the [lost & found](/lost-found/) form or bring found items to the information desk.

## Still stuck?

Email [team@berkeley.mt](mailto:team@berkeley.mt) or find any volunteer.
