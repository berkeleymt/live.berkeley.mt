## Help

{% alert(status="error", title="Emergency") %}
For an immediate emergency, **call 911** and alert the nearest BMT staff member. Emails and online forms are not monitored for emergencies.
{% end %}

### Information desk

In Dwinelle Hall by the main entrance (floor D). Come here for directions, room assignments, lost items and anything else. Ask any volunteer in a BMT shirt to point you there.

### Wi-Fi

Connect to **Berkeley-Visitor**, a free Wi-Fi service for visitors to campus. Select it from the list of networks, accept the terms of service, and you're connected; no username or password needed. For troubleshooting, read this [article](https://berkeley.service-now.com/kb/en/what-is-the-berkeley-visitor-wi-fi-network?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0014552).

### Restrooms and accessibility

The [map](/map/) marks restrooms on every floor and routes step-free when you tick "Step-free" (every tournament building has an elevator). If a route is closed or you need help, ask a volunteer or come to the information desk.

### Lost something?

Use the [lost & found](/lost-found/) form, or bring found items to the information desk.

### Getting here

Use [TakeTransit](https://taketransit.berkeley.mt/) for directions to campus by BART, bus or car.

### Still stuck?

Email [team@berkeley.mt](mailto:team@berkeley.mt) or find any volunteer.

## FAQ

{% details(title="Where do I find my room?") %}
Enter the student ID on your name tag under [My rooms](/rooms/) to see your rooms with walking directions. Your coach has the assignments too. If you still can't find it, ask any volunteer.
{% end %}

{% details(title="Where are schedule changes posted?") %}
On [updates](/updates/) and in the [schedule](/#schedule) above, where changed times are shown in red. Both refresh themselves.
{% end %}

{% details(title="Can I use a calculator?") %}
No. Calculators are not permitted on any round.
{% end %}

{% details(title="Where is lunch?") %}
Pre-ordered lunches are picked up in Kerr Lobby in the MLK Student Union. There are plenty of restaurants along Telegraph Avenue and in Downtown Berkeley.
{% end %}

{% details(title="Where do parents and coaches go during rounds?") %}
Parents and coaches are welcome in Dwinelle Hall and the MLK Student Union. Watch [updates](/updates/) for parent tours and talks.
{% end %}

{% details(title="Where is the awards ceremony?") %}
Awards happen in several auditoriums at the same time. Go to the room assigned at check-in; a different room may delay your award.
{% end %}

{% details(title="I lost something.") %}
Use the [lost & found](/lost-found/) form, or visit the information desk at Dwinelle.
{% end %}

{% details(title="Where's the information desk?") %}
In Dwinelle Hall by the main entrance (floor D). Any volunteer can point you there.
{% end %}

{% details(title="Who do I contact for an emergency?") %}
For an immediate emergency, call 911 and alert a nearby staff member. For non-immediate ones, email [team@berkeley.mt](mailto:team@berkeley.mt) or find any volunteer.
{% end %}
