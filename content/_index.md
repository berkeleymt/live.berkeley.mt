## Help

- **Emergency:** call 911, then tell any BMT staff member.
- **Information desk:** Dwinelle Hall, main entrance (floor D), for directions, room assignments and lost items.
- **Wi-Fi:** join **Berkeley-Visitor** and accept the terms; no password.
- **Restrooms and elevators:** marked on the [map](/map/); tick "Step-free" for routes without stairs.
- **Lost & found:** fill in the [form](/lost-found/), or bring found items to the information desk.
- **Anything else:** ask a volunteer in a BMT shirt, or email [team@berkeley.mt](mailto:team@berkeley.mt).

## FAQ

{% details(title="Where do I find my room?") %}
Look it up under [My rooms](/rooms/) with the student ID on your name tag. Your coach has the assignments too.
{% end %}

{% details(title="What if the schedule changes?") %}
Moved times show in red on the schedule above and are posted under [updates](/updates/); both refresh on their own. Your proctor always has the final word.
{% end %}

{% details(title="Can I use a calculator?") %}
No. Calculators aren't allowed on any round.
{% end %}

{% details(title="Where is lunch?") %}
Pre-ordered lunches are picked up in Kerr Lobby (MLK Student Union). Otherwise, Telegraph Avenue and Downtown Berkeley are a short walk.
{% end %}

{% details(title="Where do parents and coaches wait?") %}
Dwinelle Hall and the MLK Student Union are open all day. Parent talks and tours are announced under [updates](/updates/).
{% end %}

{% details(title="Where is the awards ceremony?") %}
In the auditorium assigned at check-in. Awards run in several rooms at once, so a different room may delay yours.
{% end %}

More detail, from what to bring to how awards work, is in the [tournament guide](/guide/).
