## Urgent help

For an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services and alert a nearby staff member. Online reports are not monitored for emergencies.

{{ anchor(id="wifi") }}

## Wi-Fi

Ask the information desk for the current guest network and connection instructions.

{{ anchor(id="information-desk") }}

## Information desk

Need directions, your room assignment or help with the day’s schedule? Ask a BMT volunteer to point you to the information desk.

{{ anchor(id="restrooms") }}

## Restrooms

Follow the restroom signs in your building, or ask a volunteer for the nearest accessible restroom.

{{ anchor(id="general-help") }}

## Questions or lost items

Check the [FAQ](/faq) for common questions. Lost something? [Send a private report](/lost-found) with a description and a way to reach you. Staff will contact you if they find a match.
