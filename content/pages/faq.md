## Where can I find my testing room?

Check your assigned room with your coach. [Map](/map) shows buildings and published room directions; [My rooms](/rooms) provides the available private lookup.

## Where are schedule changes posted?

Check [Live updates](/updates) and the [Schedule](/schedule). Adjusted times are labeled in the schedule. Email subscription controls are on Live updates.
