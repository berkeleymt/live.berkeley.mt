## Before you arrive

Check the [schedule](/schedule) for arrival and round times, and allow time to find your first room. Keep your student ID handy for [My rooms](/rooms).

- Bring pencils, erasers and a water bottle.
- Agree on a meeting point with your coach or family.
- Plan your journey with [TakeTransit](https://taketransit.berkeley.mt/).

{{ anchor(id="guide-finding-your-room") }}

## Finding your room

Enter your student ID in [My rooms](/rooms) to see your assigned rooms. Choose **Directions** beside a round to see its building, floor and entrance.

The [campus map](/map) helps you get your bearings. Need a step-free route or cannot find your destination? Ask a volunteer before setting off.

{{ anchor(id="guide-during-the-day") }}

## During the day

Check [live updates](/updates) for organizer announcements and schedule changes. A scheduled start is not a signal to begin a round: follow your proctor’s instructions.

Keep personal belongings with you. If you find or lose an item, use the public help tools or ask staff. Do not post personal contact details in a public description.

{{ anchor(id="guide-accessibility-and-help") }}

## Accessibility and help

Visit [Help](/help) for Wi-Fi, restrooms and the information desk. A volunteer can help with room directions or accessibility needs.

For an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services. Do not wait for an online report to be read.
