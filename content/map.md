Rounds take place in four buildings on the south side of campus. Check-in is at Dwinelle Hall. Your coach has your room assignment; the floor plans below show where each room is.

![Campus map showing Dwinelle, Wheeler, VLSB and Pauley Ballroom](/assets/campus-map.png)

| Building | Used for | Directions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dwinelle Hall** | Check-in (145 / 155), rounds, tiebreakers (229), disputes (234) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Dwinelle+Hall,+Berkeley,+CA) |
| **Wheeler Hall** | Rounds | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Wheeler+Hall,+Berkeley,+CA) |
| **Valley Life Sciences Building (VLSB)** | Rounds, awards (2050 / 2060) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Valley+Life+Sciences+Building,+Berkeley,+CA) |
| **Pauley Ballroom** (MLK Student Union) | Rounds, activities. Lunch pick-up in Kerr Lobby downstairs. | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Pauley+Ballroom,+Berkeley,+CA) |

## Floor plans

Green rooms are classrooms, blue are auditoriums. Elevators are marked ☒; ask a volunteer if you need a step-free route.

{% details(title="Dwinelle Hall — floors C, D and E") %}
Dwinelle's room numbers tell you the floor: 1xx is floor D (main entrance), 2xx is floor E, and 7x–8x is floor C below.
